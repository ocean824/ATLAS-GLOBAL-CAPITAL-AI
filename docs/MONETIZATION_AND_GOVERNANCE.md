# ATLAS Monetization & Governance

## Revenue Layers
### Founder SaaS
Free discovery/onboarding entry; paid intelligence, larger target sets, AI strategy, CRM automation, data rooms, team seats, advanced deal analysis.
### Investor SaaS
Mandate tools, global deal flow, thesis agents, team collaboration, diligence intelligence, API/export entitlements.
### Enterprise / CVC
SSO, private deployment/configuration, custom mandates, portfolio integrations, governance, audit/export, data/API contracts.
### Intelligence/API
Structured investor/company intelligence, match/routing APIs, freshness feeds, permitted analytics.
### Marketplace / Transaction Economics
Future configurable fees/success economics only after determining required registrations, licenses, exemptions, broker/dealer implications, jurisdictions, and provider constraints. Architecture remains ready; activation is gated.
### Partnerships
Disclosed referrals for legal, accounting, banking, incorporation, cloud, recruiting, data, or other services. Referral economics never alter organic capital ranking.

## Ranking Constitution
1. Organic score derives from product-defined match evidence.
2. Sponsored placement is visibly labeled and separate.
3. Investor payment cannot erase conflicts or increase evidence confidence.
4. Founder payment can unlock analysis/features, not fabricate investor affinity.
5. Material scoring changes are versioned and evaluated.
6. Admin overrides are logged.

## Founder Sovereignty Controls
Founder can define max dilution, control priorities, investor exclusions, geography preferences, strategic requirements, mission constraints, confidentiality level, and whether inbound marketplace discovery is enabled.

ATLAS may explain tradeoffs but does not silently override these preferences.

## Network Governance
As marketplace power grows, monitor concentration: share of recommendations by geography/fund, sponsored exposure, response rates, ranking diversity, systematic exclusion, and feedback loops. ATLAS's purpose is to broaden access rather than create a new opaque gatekeeper.
